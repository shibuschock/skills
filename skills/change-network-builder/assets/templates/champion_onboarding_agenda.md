**You are not:** a project manager (no deliverables or timelines), a technical SME (you don't configure or design), or a spokesperson (leadership messages come through formal channels — you reinforce and contextualize).

- What you give us: post-briefing feedback, early resistance signals, questions and gaps, success stories.
