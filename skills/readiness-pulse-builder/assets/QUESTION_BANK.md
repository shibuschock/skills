# Question Bank

~30 ready-to-use items organized by readiness dimension, plus open-text prompts. Tailor "the program" / "the new system" to the engagement's plain-language names before fielding. All scored items are Likert 1–5 (Strongly Disagree → Strongly Agree) unless noted. **(R)** = reverse-scored (high = bad signal) — use sparingly; see METHODOLOGY.md.

**Anchor picks** (one per core dimension, wording frozen across waves): AW1, UN1, LC1, CP1, ST1 — swap only with deliberate intent.

## Awareness — why the change is happening

| ID | Item | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| AW1 | I understand the objectives and purpose of the program. | Anchor — every wave |
| AW2 | I understand why the current systems and ways of working are being replaced. | Design phase |
| AW3 | I have received enough communication about the program and its timeline. | Early pulses; baseline |
| AW4 | I know who to contact if I have questions about how the change will affect my area. | Early pulses |
| AW5 | My manager has discussed the program with me or my team. (Yes/No) | First 1–2 pulses |

## Understanding — how my role and work will change

| ID | Item | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| UN1 | I am aware of how my role will be affected by the changes being introduced. | Anchor — every wave |
| UN2 | I understand what specific processes or systems will change for my role. | Design phase |
| UN3 | I have had the opportunity to provide input or feedback on the changes that affect my area. | Post-design workshops |
| UN4 | I am confident the new systems will address the challenges I face with current tools. | Mid-design through build |

## Leadership Confidence — trust in sponsors and staying power

| ID | Item | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| LC1 | I trust that leadership is making good decisions about this change. | Anchor — every wave |
| LC2 | I believe leadership will stick with this change and not abandon it partway. | Every phase; reinforcement signal |
| LC3 | Leaders in my area visibly support this change. | Build onward |
| LC4 | I feel the go-live timeline is realistic given where we are today. | Pre-go-live readiness check |

## Capability — skills, training, and support to perform

| ID | Item | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| CP1 | I feel confident I will have the skills and knowledge to do my job effectively with the new systems. | Anchor — every wave (present tense post-go-live) |
| CP2 | I know where to go to learn what I need for the new way of working. | Pre-training through go-live |
| CP3 | The training I have received (or expect to receive) is relevant to my daily work. | During/after training |
| CP4 | I know where to go for help if I have trouble after go-live. | Pre-go-live |
| CP5 | I am able to perform my core job tasks effectively using the new systems. | Post-go-live |
| CP6 | When I encounter an issue, I can resolve it or get help quickly. | Hypercare |
| CP7 | The support available during the transition has been adequate for my needs. | Hypercare |
| CP8 | I am confident my team will be fully comfortable with the new systems within the next 30 days. | Hypercare exit |

## Workload & Capacity — bandwidth to prepare and learn

| ID | Item | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| WC1 | I will have enough time during my normal workday to actually learn the new system. | Build through go-live |
| WC2 | My team has enough time to prepare for the transition alongside our regular responsibilities. | Build & test |
| WC3 | It feels like we are constantly being asked to change how we work. **(R)** | Change-saturation check — only when fatigue is suspected |
| WC4 | On my team, it is okay to be slow or make mistakes while learning something new. | Training through hypercare |

## Sentiment & Trust — buy-in, fairness, voice, trust in execution

| ID | Item | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| ST1 | I believe this change will ultimately benefit my team and our work. | Anchor — every wave |
| ST2 | I am personally on board with this change. | Every phase |
| ST3 | I am confident my area will get the same support and timing as everyone else. | Fairness/prioritization — phased rollouts |
| ST4 | I have a way to raise questions or concerns and actually be heard. | Voice — every phase |
| ST5 | I believe my area will see real value from this change on the timeline we have been told. | Trust in execution — phased rollouts, watch by segment and wave |
| ST6 | From what I have seen, I would want my team to be in the early group using the new system. | Champion recruitment / productive pull |
| ST7 | Hearing how other teams are doing with the change makes me more confident about my own. | Diagnostic: splits productive pull from anxiety |
| ST8 | I see the value of the new systems compared to the tools I used before. | 30+ days post-go-live |

## Open-text prompts (1–2 per pulse, no more)

| ID | Prompt | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| OT1 | What is your biggest question or concern about the program right now? | First pulse; phase transitions |
| OT2 | What additional support or information would help you feel more prepared for go-live? | Pre-go-live |
| OT3 | What is working well? What still needs improvement? | Go-live / hypercare |
| OT4 | If you could change one thing about how this change is being rolled out, what would it be? | Any wave with low sentiment scores |
| OT5 | What would make it easier to find time to learn the new system? | Pair with low WC1/WC2 |
