# Rollout & measurement — plan structure

`rollout_plan.md` sections, in order. Schedule **backward from go-live**.

## 1. Overview
- Go-live date(s), wave strategy (by BU / location / phase), scope (which paths/modules), key dates at a glance.

## 2. Delivery waves
| Wave | Audience(s) | Location(s) | Modules | Mode | Headcount | Window |
|------|-------------|-------------|---------|------|-----------|--------|
| 0 — Super-users | Champions across BUs | All | full + train-the-trainer | ILT | <n> | GL−6 to GL−4 wks |
| 1 | <audience> | <loc> | <module ids> | VILT/in-app | <n> | GL−3 wks |
| 2 | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

Super-users always precede their population. Respect `access_constraints` (shift work, peak business season, no-email frontline).

## 3. Logistics & dependencies
- Environment/sandbox readiness (the 70% needs somewhere to practice), device/access provisioning, facilitators, rooms/VILT platform, materials production, translation/localization lead time.
- Dependency flags (e.g. "in-app guidance requires the DAP overlay built by GL−2 wks").

## 4. Super-user / champion enablement
- Selection criteria, deeper track, train-the-trainer, their role at go-live (floor support) and after (local coaching — the 20% social band).

## 5. Learner comms touchpoints (training-specific only)
| Timing | Audience | Message | Channel |
|--------|----------|---------|---------|
| GL−3 wks | all | "Your training is coming + why" | <channel> |
| GL−1 wk | all | Schedule + pre-work | <channel> |
| GL | all | "You're go-live ready / where to get help" | <channel> |
> Broad change/stakeholder/sponsor comms live in the CIA/SHA engagement plan — reference, don't duplicate.

## 6. Readiness criteria (go/no-go training gate)
Per audience, "trained and ready" = assessment passed (L2 mastery) + practice completed + system access granted. Track % ready per wave.

## 7. Measurement plan (Kirkpatrick + adoption)
| Level | Metric | Method | Owner | Timing |
|-------|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| L1 Reaction | Usefulness/confidence | Post-session pulse | L&D | At session |
| L2 Learning | Mastery / assessment pass rate | Curriculum assessments | Facilitator | At session |
| L3 Behavior | On-the-job use vs. workaround | Observation / system usage | Ops + L&D | GL+2/4/8 wks |
| L4 Results | <CIA value lever metric> | Business data | Benefit owner | GL+ per benefit |

Tie L4 metrics to the CIA `value_levers` / `benefit_at_risk` so training evaluation connects to value realization. Name baselines/targets where known; placeholder + flag otherwise.

## 8. Reinforcement / sustainment
- Spaced microlearning + refreshers (cadence), job-aid upkeep/ownership, **new-hire onboarding** (critical for high-turnover audiences), super-user community, and how new CIA impacts re-enter the needs→curriculum→rollout loop (incremental, like the CIA update loop).

## 9. Risks & assumptions
- Training-specific risks (environment slips, facilitator capacity, peak-season scheduling) + assumptions to confirm. Flag all PLACEHOLDERs.
